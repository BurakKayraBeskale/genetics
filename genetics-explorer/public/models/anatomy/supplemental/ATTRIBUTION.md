Files are unchanged binary STL converted by the mirror from original OBJ:
- FMA7148.stl: stomach
- FMA34682.stl: acromial part of right deltoid
- FMA34683.stl: acromial part of left deltoid
- FMA37686.stl: long head of right biceps brachii
- FMA37687.stl: long head of left biceps brachii

The four muscle files are representative shoulder/upper-arm structures, not a complete muscle atlas. Coordinates are the original BodyParts3D reference frame, not registered to the HRA female atlas. Combining datasets requires explicit registration or educational representative placement; placement does not make the source anatomies a single measured subject.
